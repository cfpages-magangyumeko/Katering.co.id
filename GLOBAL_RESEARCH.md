## Purpose

This is the reusable pre-writing evidence layer for the complete `katering.co.id` catalog. It reuses authoritative research from its broader project family, then maps that evidence to this repository's frozen topic families so related sites do not repeat the same source discovery.

It is not an article, a substitute for licensed standards, an engineering calculation, or permission to publish definitive safety, legal, performance, price, or compliance claims. Article writers must cite the original sources, not this file.

## How writers must use this file

1. Start with the coverage row for the relevant topic family.
2. Open the original source before using a technical, legal, safety, product, or performance claim.
3. Treat a catalog or abstract as proof of document identity, status, and visible scope only.
4. Distinguish material, processed product, and complete installed-system performance.
5. Distinguish Indonesian requirements from foreign reference methods.
6. Preserve every limitation, stop condition, recheck trigger, and professional-review gate.
7. Do not assume that evidence shared by a related project applies to a different application, configuration, product, or jurisdiction.
8. Recheck volatile sources when outlining begins or six months after the date above, whichever comes first.

## Cross-catalog fact bank

1. A food-safety framework, a validated operator procedure, a monitoring record, and proof for one delivered batch are different evidence layers.
2. “HACCP,” “halal,” “allergen-aware,” “healthy,” “food-safe packaging,” and “certified” are scoped claims, not adjectives that can be inferred from appearance.
3. Menu quantity follows recipe, edible yield, serving unit, guest mix, attendance, service format, and measured reconciliation; there is no universal per-person number or buffer.
4. Allergen control follows ingredient identity and changes through supplier, storage, production, cleaning, packaging, service, substitution, and incident communication.
5. Nutrition-label rules for processed food do not automatically validate a nutrition number for an unpackaged catering dish.
6. Production release, holding, dispatch, route, receipt, setup, service, and leftover disposition are one evidence chain.
7. A registry entry, certificate, invoice, photograph, or logo proves only its own identity and scope; it does not prove every menu, facility, event, or date.
8. General WHO and Codex guidance supports education and control design but is not automatically Indonesian law, certification, or an operator-specific procedure.
9. Prepared-food surplus, unserved controlled surplus, plate waste, spoilage, packaging, and preparation waste require separate measurement and disposition decisions.
10. Every price, capacity, service area, lead time, menu, testimonial, certificate, facility, and delivery promise needs a dated operator-approved source.

## Source-refresh triggers

Recheck a record immediately when:

- an Indonesian law, regulation, or official standard status changes;
- a standards body publishes a new edition used by the article;
- a manufacturer changes product scope, instructions, compatibility, test evidence, or warranty;
- an article introduces a number, price, rating, classification, compliance statement, or safety procedure;
- the target project/application differs from the exemplar's jurisdiction, user, product, configuration, or operating environment.
