# Article Catalog — Katering.co.id

This catalog expands the audited catering knowledge space into distinct Indonesian article briefs. It is a planning inventory, not authorization to mass-publish. Each article must satisfy its evidence gate and editorial review before release.

Domain boundaries: Katering.co.id owns catering buyer education and its verified commercial offers. Existing first-level service routes own direct package, availability, quote, and order intent. The catalog does not create city/province/region variants, promise national fulfillment, or reserve topics away from other owned domains.

Catalog statistics: **16 parent topics, 96 article briefs, exactly 6 briefs per topic, 96 unique slugs**. Wave 1 contains 12 P0 assets. Priority means: `P0` first evidence-producing cluster; `P1` high-value next coverage; `P2` useful depth after foundations; `P3` specialist or maintenance-dependent depth.
