### KR-21

- **Original sources:** [Codex codes of practice index](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/codex-texts/codes-of-practice/en/), [General Principles of Food Hygiene, CXC 1-1969 (2022 edition)](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf).

- **Limits:** Exact critical limits, monitoring frequencies, shelf life, and corrective actions require the actual menu, recipe, equipment, process flow, hazard analysis, competent review, and current Indonesian requirements.

### KR-22

- **Original sources:** [WHO Five Keys to Safer Food Manual](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241594639).
- **Purpose for this article:** Support simple safety education while keeping it separate from a caterer's validated operating procedure.
- **Safe grounded facts:** The five keys are a communication framework; each key represents several operational controls. They do not establish a caterer's compliance, process validation, or a universal numeric limit.
- **Limits:** Do not convert the framework into exact time/temperature, cooling, reheating, transport, or shelf-life instructions without current authoritative and process-specific evidence.

### KR-23

- **Original sources:** [Codex Code of Practice on Food Allergen Management for Food Business Operators, CXC 80-2020](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/en/?lnk=1&url=https%3A%2F%2Fworkspace.fao.org%2Fsites%2Fcodex%2FStandards%2FCXC+80-2020%2FCXC_080e.pdf), [PerBPOM No. 20 Tahun 2021 official PDF](https://tabel-gizi.pom.go.id/regulasi/7_PerBPOM_Nomor_20_Tahun_2021_tentang_Perubahan_Atas_Peraturan_Badan_Pengawas_Obat_dan_Makanan_Nomor_31_tahun_2018_tentang_Label_Pangan_Olahan.pdf).
- **Purpose for this article:** Prevent “allergen-free” promises and casual substitution advice from outrunning ingredient, supplier, process, and communication evidence.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Ingredient identity, compound ingredients, supplier changes, storage, utensils, production order, cleaning, service labels, substitutions, and handoff communication can all affect allergen risk.
- **Limits:** Do not promise zero cross-contact or diagnose/treat a reaction. Recheck current Indonesian labeling scope, document the actual process, and provide a local emergency-escalation gate.

### KR-24

- **Original sources:** [PP No. 42 Tahun 2024 — BPK legal record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/304896/pp-no-42-), [BPJPH official portal](https://bpjph.halal.go.id/).
- **Purpose for this article:** Ground halal articles in the current Indonesian regime rather than logos, assumptions about ingredients, or an obsolete regulation.
- **Safe grounded facts:** A halal logo, supplier claim, or one certified ingredient does not by itself prove the entire catering service, menu, kitchen, or event process is within a current certificate's scope.
- **Limits:** Verify the certificate number, holder, scope, products/services, facilities, validity, and BPJPH record before publishing a certification or compliance claim.

### KR-25

- **Original sources:** [PerBPOM No. 10 Tahun 2026 — BPOM JDIH](https://jdih.pom.go.id/view/slide/1758/10/2026/435fc4ad37a8ea5cb40e6955a7326109).
- **Purpose for this article:** Separate regulated nutrition labeling for processed food from estimates for an unpackaged catering menu.
- **Safe grounded facts:** A regulation for processed-food labels does not automatically provide a verified calorie or nutrient value for a caterer's ready-to-eat dish. Recipe, ingredient specification, edible yield, batch variance, serving weight, method, and qualified review remain necessary.
- **Limits:** Confirm whether a product is within the rule's scope and use current laboratory/database methodology and competent nutrition review before publishing exact nutrition claims.

### KR-29

- **Original sources:** [WHO Five Keys to Safer Food Manual](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241594639), [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf), [Kemenkes ready-to-eat food-premises registry](https://tpm.kemkes.go.id/rbi/web).

- **Safe grounded facts:** Visual tidiness does not prove water quality, hidden contamination control, chemical concentration, pest history, staff competence, or record integrity.
- **Limits:** Exact chemical, concentration, contact time, sampling, PPE, or corrective-action instructions require manufacturer directions, current law, risk assessment, and competent supervision.

### KR-30

- **Original sources:** [PerBPOM No. 11 Tahun 2026 — BPOM JDIH](https://jdih.pom.go.id/view/slide/1762/11/2026/f03a9b4e99366fdc4828e6ec95408f38).
- **Purpose for this article:** Ground packaging selection in current Indonesian food-contact requirements and the actual use condition.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Food type, temperature, duration, reuse, closure, transport, leakage, migration evidence, supplier documentation, and disposal route are different selection inputs.
- **Limits:** Do not claim a material is food-safe, microwave-safe, reusable, compostable, recyclable, halal, or suitable for a menu without current product documentation and use-condition verification.

### KR-31

- **Original sources:** [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf), [WHO Five Keys to Safer Food Manual](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241594639).
- **Purpose for this article:** Connect production release to guest service instead of treating delivery speed as the only control.
- **Safe grounded facts:** A timestamp alone does not prove product temperature, container suitability, contamination control, or safe disposition after delay.
- **Limits:** Do not publish universal holding times, temperatures, delivery radii, or leftover rules without a current product/process hazard analysis, validated equipment, measurements, and Indonesian review.

### KR-34

- **Original sources:** [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf), [PP No. 86 Tahun 2019](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/129230/ppno-86-tahun-2019).
- **Purpose for this article:** Stop complaint content from substituting customer-service language for food-safety escalation.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Time, menu/item, lot, affected persons, symptoms, service point, remaining sample/product, measurements, staff, suppliers, and actions are potentially relevant records; causation cannot be inferred from chronology alone.
- **Limits:** Do not diagnose illness, assign blame, advise delay, or publish a universal notification/recall procedure. Follow the operator plan and competent Indonesian health/food authority.

### KR-35

- **Original sources:** [UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024](https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024), [Codex General Principles of Food Hygiene](https://www.fao.org/fao-who-codexalimentarius/sh-proxy/pt/?lnk=1&url=https%253A%252F%252Fworkspace.fao.org%252Fsites%252Fcodex%252FStandards%252FCXC%2B1-1969%252FCXC_001e.pdf).

- **Safe grounded facts:** Planned quantity, served quantity, unserved controlled surplus, plate waste, spoilage, packaging waste, and unavoidable preparation waste are different data streams.
- **Limits:** Do not recommend donation, reuse, cooling, reheating, animal feed, composting, or disposal for a specific item without verified history, recipient acceptance, local rules, and competent food-safety review.
